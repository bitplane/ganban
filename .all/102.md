# runtime-created columns miss links watchers

`loader.py:266` (also column.py:74) — on_links_changed is only registered on columns present at load time; create_column never attaches a watcher.

Create a column in the TUI, drag a card into it (its only column), archive the card: no watcher fires, card.archived is never set, the card disappears from the board but not into the archived view, and _recompute_blocked keeps dependents marked blocked until restart.
